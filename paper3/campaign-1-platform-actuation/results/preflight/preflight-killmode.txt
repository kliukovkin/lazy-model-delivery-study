=== interpretation (recorded at capture time, BEFORE S1 ran) ===
A) With the upstream unit exactly as shipped, the fuse-manager PID CHANGED across
   systemctl restart (2057 -> 2594). systemd KillMode=control-group SIGTERMed it;
   fusemanager/fusemanager.go:207-219 makes SIGTERM run fm.Close(ctx), unmounting
   everything; the incoming containerd-stargz-grpc then found no fuse-manager.sock,
   so StartFuseManager returned newlyStarted=true and spawned the new PID 2594.
B) With a KillMode=process drop-in the manager PID was PRESERVED (2594 -> 2594)
   while the snapshotter PID changed (2664 -> 2709) -- the intended design.

This confirms source fact (3) in ../../source-audit/S1-preregistered-predictions.md.
It does NOT by itself establish what the application sees through the mount; that is
what S1 measures.
